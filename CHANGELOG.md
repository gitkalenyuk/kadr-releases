# Changelog

What changed in the latest version of Kadr. More on the official website: https://kadr.uk/

## [1.1.15] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- Experimental, off by default: Smart search with a local model (Settings → Experimental features → Smart search). Switch it on and press Download (about 480 MB, plus 27 MB for the graphics card): Google's EmbeddingGemma 2 then runs on your computer, on the graphics card or the processor as you choose, and leaves memory when idle.
  - When the AI finds footage, Kadr checks every scene by what the picture really shows and matches scenes to each line of the narration, in any language — the AI needs to look at fewer pictures to choose.
  - Check project warns about found footage that hardly shows what is said while it plays.
  - Media: describe a moment («a chart on the screen», «a red car at night») and Kadr shows where it is in your videos and photos; one click adds the exact range to the timeline. «Find similar shots» in a clip's menu.
  - The AI finds commands, templates, effects and sounds by meaning, not only by matching words.
